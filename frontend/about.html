About Vinken Solutions

A procurement and supply partner committed to helping organizations source and receive what they need.

WHO WE ARE

Procurement Made Simple

Vinken Solutions Limited is a procurement and general supply company that helps organizations source and obtain products and services according to their specific requirements.

We work with clients who require reliable sourcing and supply solutions for their procurement needs, including tender-based requirements, Local Purchase Orders (LPOs), quotation requests and specialized product or service requirements.

Rather than limiting our clients to a fixed range of products, we work around their requirements. We identify suitable sources, coordinate procurement and arrange delivery based on the agreed specifications and terms.

Your Procurement & Supply Partner

From identifying the required products to sourcing, procurement and delivery, Vinken Solutions provides a coordinated approach to fulfilling client requirements.
